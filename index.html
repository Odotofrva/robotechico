01 / Who we are

ABOUT US

Where experimentation meets education, ownership, and elevation.

We turn curiosity into working systems — from circuits and connected devices to digital experiences.

RoboTechi is an independent creative technology lab focused on electronics prototyping, IoT, software development, and maker education. We design tools, build prototypes, and share the process so more people can create what comes next.
